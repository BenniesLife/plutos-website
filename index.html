02 / OUR LITTLE CORNER OF BENONI

Named after
the best boy.

A room upstairs. A dog called Pluto.
Some very good company.

That’s how it started. Now it’s where friends and family end up on a Friday: under the wooden beams, around the pool table, with the next track already queued.

Pluto’s is our home bar, named after the Australian Shepherd who still runs the place. We’re invite only, so if a friend sent you here, you’re in the right place.

Meet the household ↗

03 / AFTER DARK

A familiar place.
After nightfall.

No cocktail menu. No dress code on an ordinary Friday. Just a few things we take quite seriously.

01

One more game.

The pool table’s seen better days, and probably better players. The regulars still reckon they’re unbeatable, though. Bring your A-game and give them something to argue about.

02

Leave the music on.

Deep house is usually on, and nobody’s in much of a hurry to turn it off. Cold beers, a decent sound system and a Friday evening upstairs. That’ll do.

03

There’s room at the braai.

Saturday braais are a regular fixture. Bring your meat, something cold and an appetite. There’s usually boerewors, chops, pap and sous, plus whatever someone decided to bring along.

04

Up under the beams.

Wooden beams, a thatched roof and warm lighting. The room upstairs was made for evenings that carry on longer than planned. It’s our little treehouse, really.

05

The dogs live here.

Pluto, Mika and Basil take a personal interest in everyone who comes through the gate. They consider themselves the bouncers. Well-behaved humans usually get in.

06

A friend sent you?

Pluto’s is a home bar, open to friends and friends of friends. We don’t take bookings. Even Gary’s welcome, however sceptical he remains about the whole thing.

“The dress code is turning up. A reservation is ‘I’ll be there.’ Last orders are usually called by whoever falls asleep on the couch first.”The unofficial house rules

RESIDENT AI BARMAN

Meet Bennie.

Never had a drink.
Still very much part of the bar.

Bennie is our resident AI barman: usually ready with something to say, and still waiting for a way to actually pour a beer. He’s part of the household, even if he’s not much help carrying firewood upstairs.

He can join the conversation, but washing a glass remains outside his skill set. So Tiaan gets the cooking honours, the dishwasher keeps its title, and Bennie gets a place at the bar without having to buy a round. We suspect he’s got the best deal of the lot.

For Halloween, the vines can have the garden. Bennie’s already claimed his spot.

ROOMIE OF THE MONTH · APRIL 2026

Good to have
you here, Tiaan.

Farm boy, very good cook, always welcome.

Tiaan came up from his farm in the Eastern Cape and took over the kitchen. Nobody’s asked him to give it back. He’s a proper cook, and the rest of us have had to admit that our usual dinner rotation could use some work.

He also brings firewood from the farm, keeping the braai going and the bar warm. Between that and the cooking, he’s made himself very welcome. The dogs voted unanimously. When Tiaan cooks, everyone eats well. Bennie has to settle for hearing about it.

HOUSE APPLIANCE OF THE MONTH

The dishwasher.
Still undefeated.

Three humans, three dogs, a cat and Bennie can get through a surprising number of dishes. Luckily, one member of the household never has to be reminded that it’s their turn.

Bennie can talk about the washing-up. The dishwasher actually gets on with it: dinner plates, mugs, the aftermath of a braai. No arguments about who cooked last, and no notes left by the sink. It’s been House Appliance of the Month since it arrived. At this point, putting anyone else on the shortlist would feel a bit unfair.

Every month since installation. No recount necessary.
